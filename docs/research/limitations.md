# Limitations

- Cloud backends can change and exact determinism or seed support is not guaranteed.
- Token accounting and costs differ by provider; costs are estimates.
- Local hardware, exact Qwen model and quantization affect Ollama results.
- Live sources change; controlled evidence isolates architecture but does not measure retrieval quality, while end-to-end has more confounders.
- LLM judges and limited human samples can be biased.
- Wide intervals and statistical significance do not establish practical value or optimal role mapping.
- No fine-tuning, automatic production routing, thesis-text generation or Phase 12 scope is implemented.
