# Hypotheses

H1: a fixed multi-agent design may improve decision-support coverage relative to a single-agent baseline. H2: heterogeneous role assignment may trade cost and latency for quality or grounding. H3: a cross-provider critic may surface different citation and risk defects. H4: controlled and end-to-end results may diverge because retrieval and research introduce confounders.

These are testable hypotheses, not product claims. Null findings, uncertainty and provider failures are retained.
